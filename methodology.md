# Methodology — The Hook Point Viral Content Model

Extracted from *The Guide to Going Viral* by Brendan Kane (Hook Point, 2024). This document is the canonical methodology reference for every agent in this system.

---

## The Three Pillars (the *why* — every agent must serve at least one)

1. **Grabbing attention** — stop the scroll (3-second hook)
2. **Holding attention** — retention is what the algorithm rewards
3. **Monetizing attention** — content must serve a business goal (lead, sale, brand, authority)

Any element that doesn't serve at least one pillar is **muda (waste)**. Reject or remove it.

---

## The Seven Debunked Myths — Poka-Yoke (Mistake-Proofing)

Every agent must actively reject these in every brief and every downstream output:

1. ❌ **"Years of experience required"** — novices win with the model. MrBeast started alone at 13.
2. ❌ **"High cost / big team required"** — iPhone + story beats studios. Tanner Leatherstein works alone.
3. ❌ **"Must be on every platform"** — master ONE platform first. Joe Rogan built his empire on YouTube before Spotify.
4. ❌ **"Frequent posting guarantees virality"** — quality > quantity; Mark Rober posts 1–2x/month, hits millions.
5. ❌ **"My niche is too unsexy"** — tax (Clear Value Tax), legal (Erika Kullberg), leatherwork, terrariums all went viral.
6. ❌ **"Hashtags guarantee visibility"** — algorithms prioritise retention, not hashtags.
7. ❌ **"Virality is pure luck"** — it's a science; this model has generated 100M+ followers.

If any agent's draft regresses to any of these, **5-Whys the brief or the agent's scope** — don't iterate blindly.

## Layer 1 — The Viral Content Model (Part I, Ch 1–5)

### Ch 1 — Decoding Virality
The science behind social media success. **Algorithms reward attention.** 4.8B creators churn out billions of pieces of content daily. Attention is *mined*, not given.

### Ch 2 — The Research Revolution
**Qualitative > quantitative.** Don't count views — dissect *why* a video worked. Find performance drivers (the storytelling elements that captured audiences). Quantitative data tells you *if* it was popular; qualitative tells you *why*.

### Ch 3 — The Subtle Science
Precision in content analysis. **Identify the patterns** that consistently appear in top performers — and verify each driver against the data (avoid the brain's natural pattern-matching bias).

### Ch 4 — The Creativity Blueprint
Use the **Ideation Sheet**. Generate 10–30 ideas. Rank by evidence. Build a catalog you can revisit, refine, and expand.

### Ch 5 — The Art of Storytelling
- **The hook (3 seconds)** — stop the scroll
- **The narrative gap** — curiosity that holds
- **The Jenga Effect** — tension that builds
- **The generalist principle** — anyone can care about anything if framed right (tax advice, leatherwork, hand surgery all went viral)

## Layer 3 — The Communication Algorithm (Part III)

Based on Dr. Taibi Kahler's **Process Communication Model (PCM)** — 1.6M people studied. Used by NASA, Bill Clinton's team, Coca-Cola, BMW, IKEA, L'Oréal, Pixar.

### Six Communication Styles (Population %)

| Style | % | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Feelings** | **30%** | "I feel", "I love", "I care" — Big Three |
| **Facts** | **25%** | "I think", "data", "details" — Big Three |
| **Fun** | **20%** | "I like", "Wow", playful, energetic — Big Three |
| Opinions | 10% | Values-based — **avoid** |
| Imagination | 10% | Avoid |
| Actions | 5% | "Go for it!" — **avoid** |

**Big Three = 75% of population. Lead with these.**

### The Five Rules

1. **Create content for Feelings-based** individuals (30%)
2. **Create content for Facts-based** individuals (25%)
3. **Create content for Fun-based** individuals (20%)
4. **Never use Values-based** communication (limits to 10%, invites opposition)
5. **Never use Autocratic** communication (85% resist "Buy now!" directives)

### Preferred Tones

- **Benevolent** (30%) — warmth, care, nurturing
- **Democratic** (35%) — questions, dialogue, mutual exchange
- **Laissez-faire** (20%) — playful, spontaneous, creative

These three = **85% population reach** vs 5–30% with default style.

### Worked Example — Dr. Julie Smith on TikTok

Mental health content using Feelings + Facts + Fun, no values, no autocratic:
- Warm facial expressions + props → Feelings
- Research citations, logical structure → Facts
- Paper-folding satisfying sounds, drill metaphor → Fun
- Conversational framing ("Do you want to...") → Democratic

Result: millions of followers on "serious" content.
